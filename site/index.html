How this works

You rate 25 kinds of work by how much you'd want to do them.

Every job here has its own score for those same 25 things, from 0 to 4. A 0 means that kind of work isn't part of the job. A 4 means it's most of what the job is. We compare your answers to those scores and show you the closest matches. You can see the scores for any job on its card.

An AI wrote those scores, reading real job postings for each one rather than going off the job title. We haven't checked every job by hand, so treat the scores as a starting point and not the last word.

The rest of what you see on a card comes from government records: how many people the job hires in a year, how many openings are posted right now, and what education it asks for.
